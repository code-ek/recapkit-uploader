recapkit uploader

recapkit uploader is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the Twentyfifth Recaps YouTube channel and its sister channels. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does

  • Uploads videos the owner has produced to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, visibility and scheduled publish time.
  • Reads back a video's processing and visibility status to confirm the upload worked.
  • Deletes a video only when the owner asks it to (for example, a test upload).

It only acts on channels whose owner has signed in with their own Google account and granted access. It does not read anyone else's data.

See the privacy policy for how Google user data is handled and the terms of service.
